Gulf Region
Saudi Arabia's Nazaha Runs Largest Hajj-Season Integrity Sweep; Gulf Region Otherwise Quiet
The Gulf Region's most significant compliance development this week centred on Saudi Arabia's anti-corruption authority, Nazaha (Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority), which conducted approximately 15,000 inspection tours across Makkah, Madinah, and the holy sites during the 2026 Hajj season. The sweep — the largest Hajj-related integrity operation to date — resulted in investigations involving 480 government employees and the arrest of 160 individuals on corruption and misconduct charges spanning bribery and theft from public funds. The operation was conducted under the Nazaha Law enacted in 2024, which mandates immediate dismissal for any government employee found guilty of corruption. Beyond Saudi Arabia, no materially qualifying regulatory or enforcement developments were published this week from UAE or other Gulf states within the coverage window.
本周海湾地区最重要的合规动态集中于沙特阿拉伯反腐监督机构纳扎哈（Nazaha），该机构在2026年朝觐季期间在麦加、麦地那及圣地开展了约15,000次检查巡视——创历史新高。此次行动导致480名政府雇员被立案调查、160人因腐败及挪用公款等罪名被捕。该行动依据2024年颁布的纳扎哈法开展，该法规定对任何被认定存在腐败行为的政府雇员须即时免职。除沙特阿拉伯外，本周报道窗口期内未发现阿联酋或其他海湾国家有重大监管或执法公告。

Asia-Pacific

Europe
EU Sanctions Against Israeli Extremist Settlers Take Effect; Hungarian DPA Tightens Data Minimisation Standard
European compliance activity in the coverage week centred on sanctions law and data protection. The EU Council's Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1177 — designating three Israeli settler leaders and four settlement entities (Regavim, Hashomer Yosh, Amana, and the Nachala Settlement Movement) under the EU Global Human Rights Sanctions Regime — was adopted on 28 May 2026 and entered into force upon publication in the Official Journal of the EU, entering the compliance window. These designations impose asset freezes, prohibitions on making funds available, and EU travel bans; EU-regulated firms and financial institutions must immediately screen and block relevant counterparties. The EU Council also concurrently expanded its Hamas/Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ) sanctions regime to cover Politburo members and added ten individuals. In data protection, Hungary's National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH) issued a decision in late May 2026 imposing a fine for unlawful data collection at a university, establishing that data-minimisation obligations rest squarely with the controller — self-limitation by data subjects is insufficient — and that access controls cannot substitute for proper non-collection at point of intake.

Americas
OFAC Settles Russia-Sanctions Case Against FTI Consulting; Brazilian Criminal Networks Designated FTOs; Brazil Mandates Crypto AML Audits
The Americas generated significant enforcement and designations activity this week. On June 1, OFAC announced a $1.05 million settlement with FTI Consulting, Inc. for apparent violations of Russia/VTB sectoral sanctions: between 2019 and 2021, FTI indirectly extended prohibited credit to VTB Bank OAO — a sanctioned Russian state-owned entity — across six transactions by invoicing a law firm intermediary that passed payment responsibility to VTB, thereby circumventing the 14-day debt maturity limit under the Sectoral Sanctions Identifications List. The case underscores that indirect dealing structures do not insulate parties from OFAC liability. On June 5, the State Department's Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) designation of Brazilian criminal networks Comando Vermelho (CV) and Primeiro Comando da Capital (PCC) — originally announced as SDGTs on May 28 — took effect, imposing mandatory criminal penalties on material supporters and triggering heightened AML due-diligence obligations for institutions with Brazilian exposure. In Brazil, the Banco Central do Brasil's normative instruction (published May 29) requiring all cryptocurrency service providers seeking or renewing licences to submit independent audits of AML controls, fund segregation, risk management, and staff training from CVM-registered auditors entered into force on June 1.
本周美洲地区出现多项重大执法及制裁动态。6月1日，美国财政部海外资产控制办公室（OFAC）宣布与FTI Consulting Inc.达成105万美元和解协议，涉及对俄罗斯/VTB行业制裁的表面违规行为：2019至2021年间，FTI通过向律师事务所开具发票（实际付款责任转嫁至VTB银行），在六笔交易中间接向受制裁俄罗斯国有实体VTB提供超出14天到期限制的禁止信贷，构成对行业制裁名单规定的违反。此案进一步明确，间接交易架构不能规避OFAC法律责任。6月5日，美国国务部对巴西犯罪网络「红色司令」（CV）与「首都第一司令」（PCC）的境外恐怖组织（FTO）认定正式生效，将对物质支持者强制追究刑事责任，并触发对巴西业务敞口机构的强化AML尽职调查义务。在巴西，巴西中央银行5月29日发布的规范性指令要求所有申请或续期牌照的加密货币服务提供商，须提交经CVM注册审计师出具的、涵盖反洗钱控制、资金隔离、风险管理及员工培训的独立审计报告，该要求于6月1日正式生效。

Data Protection
PDPC Consults on Proposed Advisory Guidelines: Use of Personal Data in Generative AI / PDPC就生成式AI中个人数据使用发布拟议指导方针征询意见
On 2 June 2026, Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) launched a public consultation on proposed advisory guidelines governing the use of personal data in generative AI (GenAI) systems — the first Singapore framework to address privacy concerns unique to foundation-model training and GenAI applications. The guidelines address: (i) lawful bases for using personal data to train foundational models; (ii) obligations when personal data is used as input into GenAI applications; and (iii) accountability and transparency requirements for organisations deploying GenAI. Organisations are invited to submit feedback before the consultation closes. The guidelines, once finalised, will supplement existing PDPA obligations and the earlier Advisory Guidelines on AI Recommendation and Decision Systems.
2026年6月2日，新加坡个人数据保护委员会（PDPC）就生成式人工智能（GenAI）系统中个人数据使用发布拟议指导方针征询意见。这是新加坡首个专门针对基础模型训练及GenAI应用隐私风险的监管框架，内容涵盖：（一）将个人数据用于训练基础模型的合法依据；（二）将个人数据作为GenAI应用输入时的合规义务；（三）部署GenAI的组织须履行的问责制及透明度要求。征询期间，各方机构可提交反馈意见。指导方针定稿后将补充现行《个人数据保护法》义务及此前发布的人工智能推荐与决策系统指导方针。

🇯🇵 JP — Japan / 日本
Financial Regulation
Japan FSA Foreign Stablecoin Rules Enter Force: Overseas Trust-Type Stablecoins Classified as Electronic Payment Instruments / 日本金融厅境外稳定币法规正式生效：境外信托型稳定币纳入电子支付工具框架
Japan's Financial Services Agency (FSA) revised enforcement ordinance and accompanying guidelines governing the treatment of foreign stablecoins entered into force on 1 June 2026. Under the revised ordinance, trust-type stablecoins issued by overseas entities are classified as 'electronic payment instruments' under the Payment Services Act — rather than securities under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act — provided they meet specified conditions. Foreign issuers must: (i) hold an equivalent overseas regulatory licence; (ii) ensure proper collateral management and independent auditing; and (iii) be supervised by a regulator capable of cooperating with the FSA. Japanese exchanges, payment processors, and wallet providers distributing qualifying foreign stablecoins must comply with associated registration, disclosure, and user-protection obligations.
日本金融厅（FSA）修订执行令及配套指引于2026年6月1日正式生效。修订令规定，境外实体发行的信托型稳定币在满足特定条件下，将依据《支付服务法》被认定为"电子支付工具"，而非依据《金融工具和交易法》被认定为有价证券。境外发行机构须满足：（一）持有对等境外监管牌照；（二）确保适当的抵押品管理及独立审计；（三）接受能与金融厅合作的监管机构监督。在日本分发合格境外稳定币的交易所、支付处理商及钱包服务提供商须同步遵守注册、披露及用户保护等配套义务。

🇭🇺 HU — Hungary / 匈牙利
Data Protection
NAIH Rules That Data Minimisation Obligation Rests With Controller; Self-Limitation by Data Subjects Is Insufficient / 匈牙利NAIH裁定：数据最小化义务由控制者承担，不得依赖数据主体自我限制
In a decision published in May 2026, Hungary's National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH) imposed a fine on a private university that collected medical documentation from students applying for accommodation-related scholarships — data that often included highly sensitive personal information far exceeding what was strictly necessary for eligibility assessment. The NAIH held that: (i) the data minimisation obligation under Article 5(1)(c) GDPR rests squarely with the controller and cannot be discharged by allowing data subjects to choose what to submit; (ii) access controls and internal safeguards cannot substitute for proper non-collection at the point of intake; and (iii) the controller must actively design its collection processes to prevent overcollection. The decision signals heightened NAIH scrutiny of data collection practices in the education and employment sectors.
匈牙利国家数据保护与信息自由局（NAIH）于2026年5月公布的一项裁决中，对一所私立大学处以罚款。该大学在学生申请住宿奖学金时收集医疗文件，所收集数据往往包含远超资格审核所必要范围的高度敏感个人信息。NAIH裁定：（一）《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）第5条第1款（c）项规定的数据最小化义务由控制者承担，不得以允许数据主体自行选择提交内容的方式免除；（二）访问控制及内部保障措施不能弥补在源头超量收集的违规；（三）控制者须主动设计收集流程，从源头防止过度收集。此裁决预示NAIH将加强对教育及就业领域数据收集行为的审查。

Saudi Arabia's Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority (Nazaha) conducted approximately 15,000 inspection tours across Makkah, Madinah, and the holy sites during the 2026 Hajj season (late May through early June 2026) — its largest Hajj-season integrity operation on record. The sweep encompassed verification of service preparedness, compliance with operational plans, and detection of misconduct by government employees and contractors involved in Hajj service delivery. The operation resulted in suspected-case investigations involving 480 employees and the arrest of 160 individuals on charges including bribery and theft from public funds. 沙特阿拉伯监督及反腐败局（纳扎哈，Nazaha）在2026年朝觐季（5月下旬至6月初）期间在麦加、麦地那及圣地开展了约15,000次检查巡视，创历史新高。此次行动涵盖服务准备情况核查、运营计划执行合规检查，以及对朝觐服务相关政府雇员及承包商的违规行为侦查。行动结果：480名雇员被立案调查，160人因受贿及挪用公款等罪名被捕。相关行动依据2024年颁布的纳扎哈法进行，该法规定对任何被认定存在腐败行为的政府雇员须即时免职，将威慑力从财务处罚延伸至职业后果层面。

OFAC Settles With FTI Consulting for $1.05M Over Indirect Dealings With Sanctioned Russian Bank VTB / OFAC与FTI Consulting就间接与受制裁俄罗斯银行VTB交易达成105万美元和解
On 1 June 2026, the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) announced a settlement with FTI Consulting, Inc. for $1,050,000 to resolve apparent violations of the Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations. Between April 2019 and May 2021, FTI indirectly dealt in prohibited debt of VTB Bank OAO — a designated entity on OFAC's Sectoral Sanctions Identification List (SSI List) — on six occasions. FTI invoiced an intermediary law firm rather than VTB directly, but OFAC found the arrangement effectively extended credit to VTB because payment ultimately depended on the bank and the law firm did not assume the credit risk, breaching the 14-day debt maturity limit. OFAC classified the conduct as non-egregious and non-voluntarily disclosed. The case is a significant reminder that indirect dealing structures do not insulate advisory, legal, or professional services firms from OFAC liability when the economic benefit ultimately flows to a sanctioned party.
2026年6月1日，美国财政部海外资产控制办公室（OFAC）宣布与FTI Consulting Inc.达成105万美元和解协议，以解决涉嫌违反《乌克兰/俄罗斯相关制裁法规》的问题。2019年4月至2021年5月间，FTI在六笔交易中间接持有VTB银行（OAO）的被禁止债务——VTB为OFAC行业制裁识别名单（SSI名单）中的列名实体。FTI向中间律师事务所（而非直接向VTB）开具发票，但OFAC认定该安排实质上向VTB提供了信贷，因为付款最终取决于该银行，且律师事务所未承担信用风险，违反了14天债务到期期限限制。OFAC将此行为定性为非严重违规且未自愿披露。此案是对咨询、法律及专业服务机构的重要警示：当经济利益最终流向制裁对象时，间接交易结构无法规避OFAC法律责任。

On 5 June 2026, the U.S. Department of State's Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) designation of Comando Vermelho (CV) and Primeiro Comando da Capital (PCC) — two of Brazil's largest criminal networks — took effect. Banco Central Mandates Independent AML Audits for Crypto Service Providers as Licensing Condition / 巴西中央银行规定加密货币服务提供商须提交独立反洗钱审计报告作为发牌条件
A normative instruction published by the Banco Central do Brasil on 29 May 2026 entered into force on 1 June 2026, requiring all virtual asset service providers (VASPs) applying for or renewing their operational licences to submit an independent audit report as a licensing condition. The audit must be conducted by a firm registered with Brazil's securities regulator (CVM) and must cover: anti-money laundering (AML) controls and procedures, client fund segregation, risk management frameworks, and staff training programmes. The measure strengthens Brazil's VASP licensing regime — regulated by the Banco Central — and aligns with the broader FATF and COAF-driven push to bring digital asset businesses into the AML/CFT compliance framework. Non-compliant VASPs risk licence denial or revocation.
巴西中央银行于2026年5月29日发布的规范性指令于6月1日正式生效，规定所有申请或续期运营牌照的虚拟资产服务提供商（VASPs）须提交独立审计报告作为发牌条件。审计须由在巴西证券监管机构（CVM）注册的审计机构出具，并须涵盖：反洗钱（AML）控制措施及程序、客户资金隔离、风险管理框架及员工培训计划。此举强化了由巴西中央银行监管的VASP发牌制度，与金融行动特别工作组（FATF）及巴西金融情报机构（COAF）推动将数字资产业务纳入AML/CFT合规框架的整体方向一致。未达标的VASP将面临牌照被拒或被吊销的风险。
