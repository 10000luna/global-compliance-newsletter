Gulf Region  |  海湾地区
Saudi Arabia Extends Tax Amnesty and Sustains Anti-Corruption Drive as UAE Launches E-Invoicing Pilot
沙特阿拉伯延长税务特赦并持续反腐行动，阿联酋启动电子发票试点
Saudi Arabia's anti-corruption authority Nazaha reported 130 arrests and 385 active investigations in the week ending July 2, continuing its sustained enforcement drive. Separately, ZATCA extended its tax penalty exemption initiative through December 31, 2026, giving taxpayers additional relief on late filings and payments. In the UAE, the Federal Tax Authority launched a phased e-invoicing pilot program effective July 1, aimed at strengthening VAT compliance and digital tax administration. No new sanctions or AML enforcement actions met this week's verification window in the Gulf, though a CBUAE AML fine and a Saudi CMA market-integrity fine fell just outside the 7-day cutoff and were excluded from this issue.
沙特反腐败委员会（Nazaha）报告，截至7月2日的一周内共实施130次抓捕，正在进行385项调查，反腐行动持续保持高强度。另外，沙特税务与关务总局（ZATCA）将税务罚款豁免计划延长至2026年12月31日，为纳税人在逾期申报和缴款方面提供进一步宽限。阿联酋方面，联邦税务局（FTA）自7月1日起启动分阶段电子发票试点项目，旨在加强增值税合规与数字化税务管理。本周海湾地区未发现符合验证窗口的新制裁或反洗钱执法行动，但阿联酋央行的一项反洗钱罚款及沙特资本市场管理局的一项市场诚信罚款因发生时间恰好在7天窗口之外而被排除在本期简报之外。
Sources: Saudi Gazette (Jul 2, 2026, AST); Gulf News (Jul 1, 2026, GST); ZATCA official announcement
 
Asia-Pacific  |  亚太地区
Singapore and Hong Kong Sharpen Market-Conduct Enforcement as Taiwan Passes First Crypto Law
新加坡与香港强化市场行为执法，台湾通过首部加密资产专法
Singapore's Corrupt Practices Investigation Bureau (CPIB) filed new bribery charges this week, while a separate hawker-stall operator was charged with combined tax evasion and money laundering. The Monetary Authority of Singapore's Q2 enforcement roundup flagged insider-trading actions and a fine against Havenport Asset Management. In Hong Kong, the Securities and Futures Commission sharpened scrutiny of IPO bookbuilding practices ahead of a busy listing pipeline. Taiwan's legislature passed the island's first dedicated Virtual Asset Service Act, establishing a licensing and AML framework for crypto exchanges. Japan's FSA published its latest AML/CFT supervisory report, while Indonesia's KPK arrested the Regent of Langkat on bribery allegations and the tax directorate moved to freeze accounts linked to unpaid assessments. Vietnam's central bank tightened a financial-regulation directive on credit institutions. Malaysia, Thailand, and Macau were quiet this week after exhausting all available official and media sources.
新加坡贪污调查局（CPIB）本周提出新的贿赂指控；另一宗涉及一名熟食摊主的案件则同时涉嫌逃税与洗钱。新加坡金融管理局（MAS）发布的第二季度执法回顾中，披露了内幕交易执法行动以及对Havenport资产管理公司的罚款。香港方面，证券及期货事务监察委员会（SFC）加强了对首次公开招股（IPO）建簿程序的审查，显示在密集上市期间市场诚信监管趋严。台湾立法院通过了首部专门的《虚拟资产服务法》，为加密货币交易所建立牌照及反洗钱框架。日本金融厅（FSA）发布了最新的反洗钱/反恐融资监管报告；印度尼西亚反腐败委员会（KPK）以贿赂嫌疑拘捕楠榜县县长，税务总局启动冻结与欠税评估相关银行账户的程序。越南国家银行收紧了针对信贷机构的金融监管指令。马来西亚、泰国及澳门本周经详尽核查官方与媒体信源后确认无重大动态。

Europe  |  欧洲
Germany Recalibrates Bank Risk Rules as Hungary's Central-Bank Money-Laundering Probe Widens
德国调整银行风险规则，匈牙利央行洗钱调查范围扩大
Germany's banking regulator BaFin, together with the Bundesbank, issued the 9th amendment to the Minimum Requirements for Risk Management (MaRisk), updating supervisory expectations for banks' risk-management frameworks. Separately, a Frankfurt court issued a ruling in sanctions-related litigation involving Alisher Usmanov and the Dilbar yacht, addressing the scope of EU asset-freeze measures. In Hungary, the National Bank (MNB) is investigating a money-laundering case involving an estimated HUF 92 billion in flagged transactions, while authorities also reversed a controversial VAT 'M-lap' reporting rule following industry pushback.
德国联邦金融监管局（BaFin）会同德国联邦银行（Bundesbank）发布了《风险管理最低要求》（MaRisk）第九次修订，更新了对银行风险管理框架的监管期望。另外，法兰克福一家法院就涉及阿利舍尔·乌斯马诺夫（Alisher Usmanov）及其"迪尔巴尔"（Dilbar）游艇的制裁相关诉讼作出裁决，涉及欧盟资产冻结措施的适用范围。匈牙利方面，匈牙利央行（MNB）正在调查一宗涉及约920亿福林可疑交易的洗钱案件，当局同时在业界反对声浪下撤回了具争议性的增值税"M-lap"申报规则。
Sources: BaFin/Bundesbank official notice; Frankfurt Regional Court ruling; MNB Hungary official statement; KPMG Hungary alert
 
Americas  |  美洲地区
US-Mexico Sanctions Coordination Intensifies Against Cartel Finance as Brazil Strengthens AML and Market Oversight
美墨联合加大打击贩毒集团金融网络的制裁力度，巴西强化反洗钱与市场监管
The US Treasury's OFAC coordinated with Mexico's Financial Intelligence Unit (UIF) on new sanctions and account freezes targeting individuals tied to CJNG cartel finance networks. Mexican prosecutors also advanced the Edomex 'ghost payroll' corruption case, and Mexico's tax authority introduced a new biometric identification requirement for large cash transactions. In the US, the Federal Reserve issued a Prompt Corrective Action directive against a troubled bank, OFAC issued a fresh wave of Iran-related sanctions, several states enacted new consumer privacy laws, and the FTC finalized a settlement with Amazon. In Brazil, regulators across the data-protection authority (ANPD), financial-intelligence unit (COAF), securities regulator (CVM), central bank (BCB), and tax authority (Receita Federal) advanced parallel compliance actions, while Brazil also faces exposure from a separate OFAC sanctions designation.
美国财政部海外资产控制办公室（OFAC）与墨西哥金融情报局（UIF）协同行动，对与哈利斯科新一代卡特尔（CJNG）金融网络相关人员实施新制裁并冻结账户。墨西哥检方同时推进梅西哥州（Edomex）"幽灵工资"腐败案件的调查，墨西哥税务机关则推出新的大额现金交易生物识别要求。在美国，联邦储备委员会对一家问题银行发出即时纠正行动（PCA）指令，OFAC发布新一轮伊朗相关制裁，多个州通过新的消费者隐私立法，联邦贸易委员会（FTC）与亚马逊达成和解。在巴西，数据保护局（ANPD）、金融情报委员会（COAF）、证券监管机构（CVM）、中央银行（BCB）及联邦税务局（Receita Federal）多个监管机构同步推进合规执法行动，同时巴西也因另一项OFAC制裁认定而面临相关风险敞口。
