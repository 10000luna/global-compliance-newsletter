GULF REGION 海湾地区
UAE Intensifies Bank Supervision as Saudi Arabia Stays Quiet Ahead of FATF Review
The Gulf region's compliance narrative this week centered on the UAE, where the Central Bank continued a pattern of assertive supervisory enforcement against foreign bank branches, imposing a AED 1.82 million fine for failing to issue mandatory customer liability letters within the required period, in breach of its Market Conduct and Consumer Protection Regulations. The action follows a AED 20 million fine issued in late June against another foreign bank branch for AML/CFT and sanctions-screening failures, reinforcing a clear trend of tightening prudential and consumer-protection oversight as the UAE approaches its FATF Mutual Evaluation. Saudi Arabia was comparatively quiet at the primary-regulator level this week: Nazaha's most recent monthly anti-corruption disclosure fell just outside the reporting window, and no new enforcement or rulemaking activity was identified from CMA, SAMA, ZATCA or the Kingdom's data protection authority within the coverage period. Firms with Gulf operations should expect continued UAE supervisory intensity in the second half of 2026, particularly around bank governance, consumer protection and AML/CFT controls.
本周海湾地区的合规动态主要集中于阿联酋。阿联酋中央银行延续了对外资银行分行的积极监管执法态势，因一家外资银行分行未能在规定期限内出具强制性客户负债证明信、违反《市场行为与消费者保护条例》，对其处以182万迪拉姆罚款。此前该行已于6月下旬对另一家外资银行分行因反洗钱/反恐怖融资及制裁审查缺陷处以2000万迪拉姆罚款，进一步表明在阿联酋即将接受金融行动特别工作组（FATF）互评估之际，其审慎监管与消费者保护监督力度正持续收紧。相比之下，沙特阿拉伯本周在主要监管机构层面较为平静：Nazaha最近一次月度反腐败信息披露恰好发布于本报告期之前，而在报告期内，未发现来自CMA、SAMA、ZATCA或沙特数据保护主管机关的新执法或规则制定活动。在海湾地区开展业务的企业应预期阿联酋监管力度在2026年下半年将持续增强，尤其是在银行治理、消费者保护及反洗钱/反恐怖融资管控方面。

ASIA-PACIFIC 亚太地区
Coordinated Anti-Corruption Crackdowns and Market-Integrity Enforcement Sweep Across Asia-Pacific
Asia-Pacific compliance activity this week was dominated by a wave of near-simultaneous anti-corruption enforcement: Malaysia's MACC froze assets tied to an immigration-enforcement bribery syndicate, Indonesia's KPK arrested a regional government head in a sting operation over extortion of tax officials, Thailand's NACC resolved to indict a police colonel over a bail-extortion scheme, a Hanoi court opened trial against sixteen former health officials over licensing bribery, a Macau police officer was arrested for leaking enforcement intelligence to an organised vice syndicate, and Taiwan's Supreme Court finalised a bribery conviction against a former economic-development official — while Japan referred a construction company to prosecutors over alleged bribery of a Cambodian official, illustrating both intensified domestic anti-graft enforcement and growing outbound foreign-bribery exposure for regionally active firms. Market-integrity enforcement was equally active: Indonesia's OJK imposed Rp86.26 billion in capital-markets sanctions across 100 parties, Thailand's SEC moved against an investor over disclosure inconsistencies tied to a near-$1 billion True Corp stake, Vietnam's securities regulator fined a listed logistics firm for governance lapses, Taiwan prosecutors raided Evergreen Marine over suspected insider trading, and Hong Kong's SFC deepened cross-border enforcement cooperation with the CSRC. On the financial-crime and market-structure front, Singapore prosecutors added a money-laundering charge in the Nvidia AI-chip smuggling case, Thailand's central bank tightened source-of-funds disclosure for large cash deposits, Japan's FSA expanded crypto travel-rule coverage to five more jurisdictions, and Singapore's MAS and Hong Kong's HKMA advanced significant initiatives — a new protected-cell-company insurance framework and an expanded offshore RMB liquidity facility, respectively — while Hong Kong Customs also took IP-enforcement action against counterfeit-goods sellers.
本周亚太地区的合规动态以近乎同步爆发的反腐败执法浪潮为主：马来西亚反贪委会冻结了与一宗涉及执法人员的移民贿赂集团相关的资产；印尼反贪委会通过突击行动逮捕一名地方行政首长，指控其向税务官员勒索钱财；泰国国家反腐败委员会裁定起诉一名涉及保释勒索案的警察上校；河内法院开庭审理十六名前卫生官员涉嫌许可审批受贿案；澳门一名警员因向有组织卖淫集团泄露查缉情报而被捕；台湾最高法院终审确认对一名前经济发展局官员的贿赂定罪——与此同时，日本将一家建筑公司移送检方，指控其涉嫌贿赂柬埔寨官员，这既反映出区域内国内反腐执法的持续加强，也表明区域活跃企业面临日益增长的海外行贿风险敞口。市场诚信执法同样活跃：印尼金融服务管理局对100个资本市场主体实施合计862.6亿印尼盾的制裁；泰国证监会因涉及近10亿美元True Corp股权收购的披露不一致对一名投资者采取行动；越南证券监管机构因治理缺陷对一家上市物流企业处以罚款；台湾检方突击搜查长荣海运，调查其涉嫌内线交易；香港证监会则与中国证监会深化跨境执法合作。在金融犯罪与市场结构方面，新加坡检方在英伟达AI芯片走私案中加控一项洗钱罪名；泰国央行收紧大额现金存款的资金来源披露要求；日本金融厅将加密资产“旅行规则”适用范围扩大至另外五个司法辖区；新加坡金管局与香港金管局则分别推进重大举措——前者拟设立受保护单元公司保险新框架，后者扩大离岸人民币流动性安排规模；此外，香港海关亦对网售冒牌商品采取知识产权执法行动。

EUROPE 欧洲
Germany Tightens Bank Supervision and Advances EU Sanctions as Hungary Undertakes Landmark Anti-Corruption Overhaul
European compliance developments this week were led by Hungary's most significant anti-corruption institutional reform in years: the European Commission approved Hungary's accession to the European Public Prosecutor's Office, empowering EPPO to investigate EU-fund fraud and corruption allegations dating back to June 2021, while Budapest simultaneously introduced a parliamentary bill to create a new National Asset Recovery and Asset Protection Office with powers to investigate, prosecute and pursue civil recovery of improperly obtained public assets — reforms that unlocked roughly EUR 10 billion in previously withheld EU funds. In Germany, BaFin intensified supervisory action against smaller cooperative banks, fining Raiffeisenbank Lech-Donau for reporting failures and removing the management board of Raiffeisenbank Plankstetten after a special audit uncovered risk-management deficiencies, while prosecutors escalated a bribery investigation into the German Football Association over Euro 2024 ticket-allocation practices. Both jurisdictions, as EU member states, must also implement the EU's newly adopted 21st sanctions package against Russia, which extends financial-sector asset freezes to nearly 90 banks and expands shadow-fleet vessel listings and dual-use export controls — a development with direct sanctions-screening implications for German and Hungarian financial institutions and exporters.
本周欧洲合规动态以匈牙利近年来最重大的反腐败制度改革为主线：欧盟委员会批准匈牙利加入欧洲检察官办公室（EPPO），使其能够调查追溯至2021年6月的欧盟基金欺诈及腐败指控；与此同时，布达佩斯向议会提交法案，拟设立新的“国家资产追回与资产保护办公室”，赋予其调查、起诉及提起民事追回不当所得公共资产诉讼的权力——这一系列改革解冻了约100亿欧元此前被搁置的欧盟资金。在德国，联邦金融监管局（BaFin）加大了对小型合作银行的监管执法力度，因报告违规对莱希-多瑙河莱夫艾森银行处以罚款，并在专项审计发现风险管理缺陷后撤换了普兰克施泰滕莱夫艾森银行的董事会；同时，检方就德国足协涉2024年欧洲杯门票分配的行贿调查进一步升级。作为欧盟成员国，德国与匈牙利均须落实欧盟新通过的第21轮对俄制裁措施，该措施将金融部门资产冻结范围扩大至近90家银行，并新增“影子船队”船只清单及扩大军民两用出口管制——这对两国金融机构及出口商的制裁审查义务具有直接影响。
Sources: European Commission (10 Jul 2026, CEST); BaFin (9–10 Jul 2026, CEST); Staatsanwaltschaft Bochum/LKA NRW (1–7 Jul 2026, CEST); European Council (13 Jul 2026, CEST).
AMERICAS 美洲
US Advances Coordinated AML Reform and High-Profile Trade-Secret Litigation as Mexico and Brazil Ramp Up Data-Protection Enforcement
The United States saw the most extensive compliance activity of any single jurisdiction this week, spanning nearly every topic area: the Federal Reserve proposed a risk-based AML/CFT program rule for supervised banks in tandem with FinCEN's broader Anti-Money Laundering Act reform, OFAC issued new Iran-related and counter-terrorism sanctions designations targeting shadow-banking networks, the FTC settled Fair Credit Reporting Act allegations against a tenant-screening firm, Apple filed a high-profile trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI alleging systematic theft of hardware IP, a federal court rejected Kalshi's bid to block New York's gambling-law enforcement against its prediction-market contracts, the SEC unveiled a 2026 regulatory agenda prioritising a crypto safe-harbor rulemaking, and Treasury/IRS finalised rules targeting abusive charitable-trust tax arrangements — alongside a federal public-corruption guilty plea from Jackson, Mississippi's former mayor. Mexico and Brazil both showed a parallel intensification of data-protection enforcement: Mexico's post-INAI regulator fined the Mexican Football Federation 42.8 million pesos for unlawful biometric-data processing under its FAN ID stadium system, while Brazil's ANPD opened a formal sanctioning process against a health-sector nonprofit over LGPD compliance failures tied to a breach affecting 500,000 patients. Mexico also advanced AML cooperation between its Financial Intelligence Unit and consumer-protection regulator ahead of the 2026 World Cup, continued a legacy Pemex-era bribery prosecution, and issued a wave of tax and financial-regulation updates, while Brazil's Federal Police executed a major anti-corruption sweep blocking roughly R$100 million tied to misappropriated congressional earmark funds.
本周美洲地区中，美国的合规活动最为密集，几乎覆盖所有议题领域：美联储提出基于风险的银行反洗钱/反恐怖融资项目新规，与金融犯罪执法网络（FinCEN）更广泛的《反洗钱法》改革相配合；美国海外资产控制办公室（OFAC）发布新一轮伊朗相关及反恐制裁名单，剑指影子银行网络；联邦贸易委员会（FTC）就一家房客背景调查公司违反《公平信用报告法》的指控达成和解；苹果公司对OpenAI提起备受瞩目的商业机密诉讼，指控其系统性窃取硬件知识产权；联邦法院驳回Kalshi阻止纽约州对其预测市场合约实施博彩法执法的请求；美国证券交易委员会（SEC）公布2026年监管议程，将加密资产安全港规则制定列为优先事项；财政部与国税局最终确定针对滥用型慈善信托税务安排的规则——与此同时，密西西比州杰克逊市前市长也已就联邦公共腐败指控认罪。墨西哥与巴西同步加强数据保护执法：墨西哥“后INAI”监管机构因Fan ID球场系统非法处理生物识别数据，对墨西哥足球协会处以4280万比索罚款；巴西国家数据保护局（ANPD）则对一家医疗行业非营利组织启动正式处罚程序，原因是其在一宗影响50万名患者的数据泄露事件中未能履行《通用数据保护法》（LGPD）合规义务。此外，墨西哥金融情报署与消费者保护监管机构在2026年世界杯前深化反洗钱合作，继续推进Pemex时代遗留贿赂案的起诉，并发布了一系列税务及金融监管更新；巴西联邦警察则展开重大反腐行动，冻结约1亿雷亚尔与国会拨款挪用相关的资金。
