UAE Central Bank Delivers Record AML Penalty as Gulf Braces for FATF Scrutiny

🇮🇩 ID — Indonesia 印度尼西亚
Market Integrity
OJK Enforces New Rules on Financial Influencers: Licensing, Disclosure and Account-Block Powers
OJK执行金融信息提供者新规：许可证要求、信息披露义务与封号权力
Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) held a press briefing on 24 June 2026 to publicise the enforcement mechanisms of POJK No. 6 of 2026 on the Conduct of Financial Sector Information Providers, enacted on 4 June 2026. The regulation targets financial influencers ('finfluencers') recommending capital market, banking, and digital financial products. Key requirements include: (i) influencers recommending regulated products (e.g., investment adviser licence for capital market products) must hold the relevant professional licence; (ii) those promoting digital assets must hold competency certification; (iii) paid partnerships or sponsored content must be clearly disclosed; (iv) digital asset recommendations are restricted to products listed on OJK-authorised exchanges. Violations may result in written warnings, licence revocation, or fines of up to IDR 15 billion (approx. USD 920,000). OJK may also request the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs to block, suspend, or remove violating accounts and content.
2026年6月24日，印度尼西亚金融服务管理局（OJK）召开新闻发布会，宣传于2026年6月4日正式颁布的《2026年第6号OJK条例》（POJK No. 6 of 2026《金融领域信息提供者行为准则》）的执法机制。该条例主要针对在社交媒体上推荐资本市场、银行及数字金融产品的'金融网红'。核心要求包括：（一）推荐受监管产品（如资本市场产品）的信息提供者须持有相应专业执照（如投资顾问许可证）；（二）推广数字资产者须持有资质认证；（三）付费合作或赞助内容须明确披露；（四）数字资产推荐须限于OJK授权交易所上架的产品。违规者将面临书面警告、吊销执照或最高150亿印尼盾（约合92万美元）罚款。OJK还可向通信和数字事务部申请对违规账号和内容采取访问屏蔽、账号暂停或删除内容等措施。

OJK Crypto Regulation Advances Toward MiCA-Aligned Deadline in July 2026
OJK加密监管向MiCA对齐的7月2026年期限推进
Indonesia's OJK is advancing its crypto-asset regulation framework toward a July 2026 compliance deadline that aligns with principles comparable to the EU's MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets) regulation. 🇹🇭 TH — Thailand 泰国
AML / CFT
SEC Thailand Issues Landmark AML Guidelines for Capital Market Intermediaries (Effective 16 Aug 2026)
泰国证交会发布资本市场中介机构反洗钱里程碑指南（2026年8月16日起生效）
Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) published new AML/CFT guidelines in June 2026 for all capital market intermediaries, effective 16 August 2026, in what the regulator describes as 'one of its most significant anti-financial crime initiatives in recent years.' The guidelines require capital market intermediaries to: (i) strengthen customer due diligence (CDD), including beneficial ownership verification; (ii) enhance transaction monitoring to trace deposits and withdrawals back to their source, including through bank accounts registered in the same name as the customer; and (iii) implement robust screening and controls for wire transfers. The guidelines were developed through collaboration between the SEC, the Anti-Money Laundering Office (AMLO), the Central Investigation Bureau, and industry participants, reflecting a coordinated effort to close loopholes exploitable by fraudsters, money launderers, and cybercriminals operating through Thailand's capital markets.
2026年6月，泰国证券交易委员会（SEC）发布适用于所有资本市场中介机构的新版反洗钱/反恐融资指南，自2026年8月16日起生效。SEC秘书长将此次指南形容为'近年来最重要的反金融犯罪举措之一'。新指南要求资本市场中介机构：（一）强化客户尽职调查（CDD），包括受益所有权核查；（二）加强交易监控，要求存款和提款可追溯至资金来源，包括通过与客户同名注册的银行账户；（三）对电汇交易实施严格的筛查和控制措施。该指南由SEC、反洗钱办公室（AMLO）、中央调查局及行业参与方联合制定，旨在通过协调配合堵塞欺诈者、洗钱者及网络犯罪分子可能利用泰国资本市场的各类漏洞。

🇻🇳 VN — Vietnam 越南
Financial Regulation
State Bank of Vietnam Issues Circular 09/2026/TT-NHNN Modernising Securities Custody Framework (Effective 4 Jul 2026)
越南国家银行颁布《2026年第09号通知》现代化证券托管框架（2026年7月4日起生效）
The State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) issued Circular No. 09/2026/TT-NHNN regulating the custody and use of securities at the State Bank of Vietnam, effective 4 July 2026. The circular replaces earlier securities custody regulations and aims to: improve the legal framework governing the custody and handling of valuable papers; ensure compliance with newly enacted financial regulations; and enhance digital transformation and administrative efficiency.
